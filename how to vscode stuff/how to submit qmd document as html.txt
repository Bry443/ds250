WITHIN VS CODE:
1. open a new Powershell terminal
2. enter: quarto render
3. from lefthand vscode Explorer, drag the .qmb file into terminal (this pastes the file reference)
4. enter, and you should now have the html in your workspace folder.



Make sure that your YAML header at the top of your .qmd file has:
embed-resources: true

	example:
title: "Client Report - Project 0: Introduction"
subtitle: "Course DS 250"
author: "[STUDENT NAME]"
format:
  html:
	self-contained: true
	V v V v V v
	embed-resources: true
	^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^
	page-layout: full
	title-block-banner...
